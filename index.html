Chip and crack repair

Chips smaller than a quarter and cracks under six inches can often be repaired, especially outside the driver's line of sight. A repair keeps the original factory seal and is usually quicker and less expensive than replacing.

Lane departure warning, automatic braking and other driver-assistance cameras and sensors can need recalibration after windshield work. We handle it in-house so your safety systems read the road correctly.

FAQ

Common questions

Should I repair or replace my windshield?

It depends on the size and location of the damage. Chips smaller than a quarter and cracks under six inches can often be repaired, especially if they are not in the driver's line of sight. Side, rear and sunroof glass is usually tempered and must be replaced. A technician can inspect it and recommend the safest, most cost-effective option.

Will my insurance cover it?

Most comprehensive policies cover glass repair and replacement, but details vary. Many insurers waive the deductible for windshield repairs. For a full replacement your deductible may apply unless you have full glass coverage. Check with your provider before scheduling.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

Typically about 60 to 90 minutes, then a curing period of about an hour before the vehicle should be driven. ADAS recalibration, if needed, can extend the appointment.

Is aftermarket glass safe, or do I need OEM?

Both OEM and high-quality aftermarket glass can be safe when installed correctly. Aftermarket quality varies. On newer vehicles with ADAS features, OEM glass is sometimes recommended for best sensor performance and calibration.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. Our fully equipped mobile team can complete repairs and replacements at your home or workplace, at no extra charge.
